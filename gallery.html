Life at SCC

A Glimpse Into
the Canyons

Every day looks a little different at SCC. Here’s what learning in the canyons actually looks like.

Campus Friends

Meet the Animals

Two goats, a miniature horse, a tortoise, and a leopard gecko call SCC home. Caring for them is part of the curriculum every day.

Get Involved

Interested in Volunteering with Our Animals?

We occasionally welcome community members to help care for our campus animals. If you’d like to be considered, drop us a note and Laurie will be in touch.

Wild Neighbors

Wildlife & Visitors

Canyon life means constant wildlife encounters — monarch caterpillars on milkweed, dragonflies landing on outstretched hands, toads found after the rain, and birds nesting in the walls of the school.

Hands-On Learning

Learning Through Play

No worksheets here. Mud, paint, dough, and water are our materials. Getting thoroughly messy is encouraged.

Nature & Collaboration

Making Things Together

Children arranging sticks, stones, and seed pods around the stump. Carrying a huge branch across the yard together. Building a brick wall from scratch. This is what emergent curriculum looks like.

How We Learn

Emergent Curriculum

When children get interested in something — measuring rocks, pressing bugs into clay, building ramps, exploring light — teachers follow that curiosity. What looks like play is exactly how learning happens.

Field Trips

Silverado Creek

Silverado Creek winds through the canyon just beyond the school. Kids wade in, study the water, and wade some more. Rain or shine, creek days are the best days.

Where We Learn

Our Campus

Nestled in the canyons of Silverado, the campus blends the built and natural worlds — tire swings, garden beds, the Hawk Tree, and miles of trails just beyond the fence.

Beyond the Fence

Life in the Canyons

Every morning begins with a hike. The canyon is the classroom — creeks, trails, wildlife, and the open sky overhead.
